genial

[ˈdʒiːniəl]

📖 释义

adj. 友好的，愉快的；温和的，温暖的

adj. friendly and cheerful; pleasantly mild and warm

🖼️ 图像联想

A person with arms spread wide under golden sunshine

💬 例句

The genial celebration brought the whole community together in joy.
