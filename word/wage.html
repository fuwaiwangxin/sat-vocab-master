wage

[weɪdʒ]

📖 Definition

n. a fixed regular payment earned for work or services, typically paid on a daily or weekly basis v. carry on (a war or campaign)

🖼️ Picture This

Hands reaching down to pull someone up from a ledge

💬 Example Sentence

Volunteers worked to wage the relief efforts in the disaster zone.

📝 Word Roots & Related

wage a war
